How it works

Route

Verify

Questions

Frequently asked

Who is Jurisdicta for?

Independent advocates, law chambers, mid-sized firms, and in-house legal teams, starting in Bangladesh.

Is the product available today?

Not publicly yet. We’re in the early research stage and talking to lawyers to shape what we build. Request early access and we’ll reach out as pilots open.

Does Jurisdicta give legal advice?

No. It’s a tool for lawyers. Drafts, analyses, and answers are meant to speed up your work and must be reviewed by you.

How do I know an AI answer is right?

Answers cite the document and page they came from, so you can check the source directly instead of taking the output on trust.

Will it work with Bangla documents?

Local-language support is a priority for our Bangladesh launch. We’re validating exactly which document types and languages lawyers need first, and your input helps decide that.

Can I help shape the product?

Yes, please. We’re looking for practising lawyers for short interviews and early pilots. Use the form below.
